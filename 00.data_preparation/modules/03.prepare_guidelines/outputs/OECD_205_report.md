## Purpose and Scope

This test guideline is designed to determine the median lethal concentration (LC50) of a chemical in the diet of birds following a five-day dietary exposure followed by a recovery period. It is used to assess the acute toxicity of chemicals to avian species, particularly for environmental risk assessment of pesticides and industrial chemicals. The test cannot be used for highly volatile substances or those that do not mix uniformly in the diet.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Signs of intoxication and abnormal behavior (recorded twice on day 1, daily thereafter).
- Mortality (recorded twice on day 1, daily thereafter).
- Body weights (recorded on day 0, 5, 8, and end of test if extended).
- Food consumption (measured for periods 0-5, 5-8, and 8-end of test).
