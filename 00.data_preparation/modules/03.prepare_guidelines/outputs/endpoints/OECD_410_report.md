## Purpose and Scope

OECD 410 is used to determine subchronic dermal toxicity following repeated daily topical application of a substance over 21 or 28 days. It is applied after initial information has been obtained from acute testing and provides information on possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated dermal exposure. The study informs selection of dose levels for longer-term studies and provides data on the degree of percutaneous absorption.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Careful clinical examination at least once each day
- Cage-side observations including: changes in skin and fur, eyes and mucous membranes, respiratory, circulatory, autonomic and central nervous system, somatomotor activity and behaviour pattern
- Signs of toxicity recorded with time of onset, degree, and duration
- Food consumption measured weekly; animals weighed weekly
- Skin application site assessed throughout study

### Haematology
- Evaluated at end of the test period
- Haematocrit, haemoglobin concentration, erythrocyte count, total and differential leucocyte count
- Measure of clotting potential: clotting time, prothrombin time, thromboplastin time, or platelet count

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Evaluated at end of the test period (baseline data considered if historical data inadequate)
- Calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium, fasting glucose
- Alanine aminotransferase (ALT), aspartate aminotransferase (AST)
- Ornithine decarboxylase, gamma glutamyl transpeptidase
- Urea nitrogen, albumin, blood creatinine, total bilirubin, total serum protein
- Additional as needed: lipids, hormones, acid/base balance, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase activity
- Urinalysis only when indicated by expected or observed toxicity

### Pathology
- Full gross necropsy for all animals: external surface, all orifices, cranial, thoracic and abdominal cavities
- Organs weighed wet: liver, kidneys, adrenals, testes
- Tissues preserved for histopathology: normal and treated skin, liver, kidney, and target organs (those showing gross lesions or changes in size)
- Histopathology performed on high dose and control groups; extended to lower dose groups if necessary
- Satellite group examined histologically with emphasis on organs showing effects in treated groups
