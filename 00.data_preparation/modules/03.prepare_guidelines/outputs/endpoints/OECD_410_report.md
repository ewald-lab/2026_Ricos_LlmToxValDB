## Purpose and Scope

This guideline is used for the assessment and evaluation of the toxic characteristics of a chemical by determining subchronic dermal toxicity after initial information on toxicity has been obtained by acute testing. It provides information on possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated exposures by the dermal route over a limited period of time (21 or 28 days). The main difference between the two durations covered is simply the time over which dosing takes place.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Daily careful clinical examination.
- Cage-side observations including changes in skin and fur, eyes, mucous membranes.
- Respiratory, circulatory, autonomic, and central nervous system signs.
- Somatomotor activity and behaviour pattern.
- Weekly measurements of food consumption and body weight.
- Recording of signs of toxicity (onset, degree, duration).

### Haematology
- Haematocrit.
- Haemoglobin concentration.
- Erythrocyte count.
- Total and differential leucocyte count.
- Measure of clotting potential (clotting time, prothrombin time, thromboplastin time, or platelet count).
*Note: Investigations performed at the end of the test period.*

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Liver function parameters.
- Kidney function parameters.
- Suggested determinations: Calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium, fasting glucose, serum glutamic-pyruvic transaminase (ALT), serum glutamic-oxaloacetic transaminase (AST), ornithine decarboxylase, gamma glutamyl transpeptidase, urea nitrogen, albumen, blood creatinine, total bilirubin, total serum protein.
- Additional analyses may include lipids, hormones, acid/base balance, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase activity.

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Full necropsy of all animals (external surface, all orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities).
- **Organ Weights:** Wet weights of liver, kidneys, adrenals, and testes must be taken immediately after dissection.
- **Histopathology:** Examination of preserved organs/tissues from high dose and control groups (skin, liver, kidney, target organs). Extended to other groups if necessary. Satellite group examined with emphasis on affected organs.
