## Purpose and Scope

OECD 440 is a short-term in vivo screening test designed to evaluate the ability of a chemical to elicit oestrogenic agonist (or antagonist) activity via the uterotrophic response — a statistically significant increase in uterine weight relative to vehicle controls. The assay exploits the low circulating oestrogen state in either immature female rodents (prior to puberty) or ovariectomised adult females, providing a sensitive baseline for detecting oestrogen-responsive uterine growth. It is positioned at Level 3 of the OECD Conceptual Framework for Endocrine Disrupter Testing and Assessment, providing mechanistic data about a single endocrine endpoint (oestrogenicity). Two validated methods are available: (i) the immature non-ovariectomised method (preferred for animal welfare reasons) and (ii) the ovariectomised adult female method. Both methods have comparable sensitivity and reproducibility as demonstrated in the OECD validation programme. The immature method covers a broader scope of investigation (responding to HPG-axis interactions) but is somewhat less specific; the OVX method responds only to oestrogen receptor-mediated activity.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily; more frequently when signs of toxicity are observed; performed at the same time(s) each day, accounting for anticipated peak effects after dosing
- Observations include: mortality, morbidity, changes in behaviour, skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions, and autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, unusual respiratory pattern)
- All animals weighed daily to nearest 0.1 g, starting just prior to treatment initiation
- Food consumption (grams per animal per day) measured per cage as an optional measurement

### Haematology
- Not required in this guideline

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Not required in this guideline

### Pathology
- All animals humanely killed approximately 24 hours after the last dose; necropsy order randomized across groups
- **Primary endpoint -- uterine weights (both):**
  - **Wet uterine weight** (uterus + luminal fluid contents) measured to nearest 0.1 mg
  - **Blotted uterine weight** (luminal fluid expressed and removed) measured to nearest 0.1 mg
- Vaginal opening status examined before dissection in immature animals
- Uterine horns and ovaries detached; excess fat and connective tissue trimmed; vagina separated just below cervix (cervix remains with uterus)
- In OVX animals: ovarian stubs examined at necropsy for presence of any residual ovarian tissue; animals with ovarian tissue excluded from calculations
- **Optional investigations:** Histopathological examination of uterus fixed in 10% neutral buffered formalin (H&E staining); vaginal histology for keratinization/cornification; morphometric measurement of endometrial epithelium height
- Positive response: statistically significant (p < 0.05, one-tailed) increase in mean uterine weight (wet and/or blotted) in treated group vs. vehicle control
- Reference oestrogen for laboratory proficiency verification: 17alpha-ethinyl estradiol (EE)
