## Purpose and Scope

This guideline describes two methods for detecting skin sensitisation potential in guinea pigs: the Guinea Pig Maximisation Test (GPMT) and the Buehler Test. The GPMT uses Freund's Complete Adjuvant to potentiate the immune response, while the Buehler test is a non-adjuvant method. These tests are intended to be used as a last resort for animal welfare reasons if other in chemico, in vitro, or in vivo methods (like LLNA) are not applicable. They provide qualitative data on sensitisation potential and limited quantitative potency information.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   All skin reactions (erythema, oedema) at induction and challenge sites.
*   Systemic reactions resulting from induction and challenge procedures.
*   Unusual findings during the observation period.
*   Grading of reactions using the Magnusson and Kligman scale (0-3).

### Haematology
*   Not typically required for this specific endpoint-focused guideline (primary endpoint is dermatological).

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Not typically required for this specific endpoint-focused guideline.

### Pathology
*   Histopathological examination may be carried out to clarify doubtful reactions.
*   Measurement of skin fold thickness may be carried out to clarify doubtful reactions.
*   Any histopathological findings must be reported if performed.
