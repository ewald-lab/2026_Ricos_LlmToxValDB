## Purpose and Scope

OECD TG 452 provides information on the possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated chemical exposure over a considerable part of the lifespan of the test species. The study identifies the chronic toxicity of a chemical, target organs, and characterises the dose-response relationship. It provides an estimate of the no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) or point of departure for establishment of a Benchmark Dose (BMD), predicts chronic toxicity effects at human exposure levels, and supplies data to test hypotheses regarding mode of action. Originally adopted in 1981, TG 452 was revised in 2018 in parallel with TG 451 (Carcinogenicity Studies) and TG 453 (Combined Chronic Toxicity/Carcinogenicity Studies) to reflect developments in animal welfare and regulatory requirements, and is designed for use with a broad range of chemicals including pesticides and industrial chemicals.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Morbidity/mortality checks at the beginning and end of each day, including weekends and holidays (par. 34)
- General clinical observations at least once daily, preferably at the same time(s) each day, accounting for peak effects post-gavage (par. 34)
- Detailed clinical observations on all animals at least once before first exposure, at end of week 1, and monthly thereafter; performed outside the home cage in a standard arena at consistent times (par. 35)
- Signs include (but are not limited to): changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions, autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, unusual respiratory pattern); gait, posture and response to handling; clonic or tonic movements; stereotypies (excessive grooming, repetitive circling) or bizarre behaviour (self-mutilation, walking backwards) (par. 35)
- Ophthalmological examination (ophthalmoscope or equivalent) on all animals before first administration; at termination preferably in all animals but at minimum in high dose and control groups; all animals examined if treatment-related ocular changes detected (par. 36)
- Optional neurotoxicity assessments (if previous 28-day/90-day studies indicated neurotoxic potential): sensory reactivity to auditory/visual/proprioceptive stimuli, grip strength, motor activity -- before study commencement and at 3-month intervals up to 12 months, and at study termination if longer than 12 months (par. 37)
- Optional immunotoxicity investigations (if previous studies indicated immunotoxic potential): conducted at termination (par. 38)
- Body weight: at start of treatment, at least weekly for the first 13 weeks, then at least monthly (par. 39)
- Food consumption and food efficiency: at least weekly for the first 13 weeks and at least monthly thereafter (par. 39)
- Water consumption: at least weekly for the first 13 weeks and at least monthly thereafter when the substance is administered in drinking water; also considered when drinking activity is altered (par. 39)

### Haematology
- At least 10 males and 10 females per group at 3, 6, and 12 months, and at study termination (if longer than 12 months), using the same animals throughout; satellite animals may be needed in mice (par. 40)
- 3-month sampling may be omitted if no haematological effects were seen in a prior 90-day study at comparable dose levels (par. 40)
- Blood samples taken from a named site (e.g., cardiac puncture or retro-orbital sinus) under anaesthesia (par. 40)
- Parameters (par. 41): total and differential leukocyte count, erythrocyte count, platelet count, haemoglobin concentration, haematocrit (packed cell volume), mean corpuscular volume (MCV), mean corpuscular haemoglobin (MCH), mean corpuscular haemoglobin concentration (MCHC), prothrombin time, activated partial thromboplastin time
- Additional parameters as appropriate: Heinz bodies or atypical erythrocyte morphology, methaemoglobin (par. 41)
- If haematopoietic effects observed: reticulocyte counts and bone marrow cytology (not routine) (par. 41)

### Clinical Biochemistry
- At least 10 males and 10 females per group at 3, 6, and 12 months (same animals as haematology); overnight fasting recommended (except mice) (par. 42)
- 3-month sampling may be omitted if no clinical biochemistry effects were seen in a prior 90-day study at comparable dose levels (par. 42)
- Parameters (par. 42): glucose, urea (urea nitrogen), creatinine, total protein, albumin, calcium, sodium, potassium, total cholesterol
- At least 2 hepatocellular tests (chosen from: alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, glutamate dehydrogenase, total bile acids) (par. 42)
- At least 2 hepatobiliary tests (chosen from: alkaline phosphatase, gamma glutamyl transferase, 5'-nucleotidase, total bilirubin, total bile acids) (par. 42)
- Optional: fasting triglycerides, specific hormones, cholinesterase -- depending on known/expected toxicity (par. 42)
- Urinalysis at same intervals on at least 10 males and 10 females per group (par. 43): appearance, volume, osmolality or specific gravity, pH, total protein, glucose
- Additional urinalysis: ketone, urobilinogen, bilirubin, occult blood (par. 43)

### Pathology
- Full detailed gross necropsy for all animals: external surface, all orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities and contents; provision may be made for interim/satellite groups to restrict to key measures (par. 45)
- Organ weights (all animals except moribund/intercurrently killed) (par. 46): adrenals, brain, epididymides, heart, kidneys, liver, ovaries, spleen, testes, thyroid (post-fixation, with parathyroids), uterus; adrenal glands optional in mice
- Tissues preserved for histopathology (items in brackets are optional) (par. 47): all gross lesions; adrenal gland; aorta; brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla/pons); caecum; cervix; coagulating gland; colon; duodenum; epididymis; eye (including retina); [femur with joint]; gall bladder (non-rat species); Harderian gland; heart; ileum; jejunum; kidney; lacrimal gland (exorbital); liver; lung; lymph nodes (superficial and deep); mammary gland (obligatory for females; males if visible); [upper respiratory tract: nose, turbinates, paranasal sinuses]; [olfactory bulb]; oesophagus; ovary; pancreas; parathyroid gland; peripheral nerve; pituitary; prostate; rectum; salivary gland; seminal vesicle; skeletal muscle; skin; spinal cord (cervical, mid-thoracic, lumbar); spleen; [sternum + bone marrow section/aspirate]; stomach (forestomach + glandular); [teeth]; testis; thymus; thyroid; [tongue]; trachea; urinary bladder; uterus (including cervix); vagina; [ureter]; [urethra]
- For paired organs (e.g., kidneys, adrenals): both organs preserved and examined
- Minimum histopathology (par. 48): all tissues from high dose and control groups; all tissues from animals dying/killed during the study; all tissues with macroscopic abnormalities; target tissues from all dose groups when treatment-related changes identified in high dose group; paired organs both examined
