## Purpose and Scope

This guideline provides information on possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated exposure over a prolonged period (90 days), covering post-weaning maturation and growth into adulthood. It aims to identify major toxic effects, indicate target organs, assess accumulation potential, and estimate a no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL). The updated guideline places additional emphasis on endocrine-sensitive endpoints (thyroid hormones, cholesterol profiles) to improve the detection of potential endocrine activity.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations made at least once daily.
- Detailed clinical observations (outside home cage) at least once prior to first exposure and weekly thereafter.
- Signs include changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, autonomic activity, gait, posture, and behavior.
- Ophthalmological examination prior to administration and at termination (all animals preferred, minimum high dose and control).
- Functional observations towards the end of the study: sensory reactivity, grip strength, and motor activity assessment.

### Haematology
- Haematocrit
- Haemoglobin concentration
- Erythrocyte count
- Reticulocyte count
- Total and differential leukocyte count
- Platelet count
- Blood clotting time/potential measure

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Electrolytes: Sodium, Potassium
- Metabolic markers: Glucose, Urea, Blood urea nitrogen, Creatinine
- Proteins: Total protein, Albumin
- Lipids: Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL
- Enzymes (Hepatocellular): ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, Sorbitol dehydrogenase
- Additional optional: Calcium, Phosphorus, Fasting triglycerides, Bilirubin, Bile acids, Methaemoglobin, Cholinesterase
- **Endocrine Specific:** Serum T4, T3, TSH (Required); Testosterone, Estradiol, FSH, LH (Optional/Case-by-case)

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, orifices, and cavities.
- **Organ Weights:** Liver, kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, prostate + seminal vesicles with coagulating glands, uterus, ovaries, thymus, spleen, brain, heart, pituitary, thyroid.
- **Histopathology:** All gross lesions; full histopathology on control and high-dose groups for all preserved tissues (brain, spinal cord, pituitary, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, GI tract, liver, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, lungs, reproductive organs, etc.). Extended to lower doses if treatment-related changes are observed.
- **Reproductive:** Oestrus cycle determination (vaginal smears) for females; sperm reserves/morphology/motility (optional) for males.

### Footnotes

*   **Footnote 1:** For glucose measurements, overnight fasting is preferable to reduce variability, though it may disturb daily exposure in feeding studies.
*   **Footnote 2:** Thyroid trimming and weighing should be done very carefully, preferably after fixation, to avoid tissue damage which compromises histopathology.
*   **Footnote 3:** Control hormone levels must be taken into account to differentiate between incidental and treatment-related changes due to kit variability and biological factors (diurnal variation, oestrus stage).
*   **Footnote 4:** Laboratories should strive to keep control coefficients of variation below 25% for T3/T4 and below 35% for TSH.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Body weight and body weight changes
- Food consumption and water consumption (if applicable)
- Signs of toxicity (onset, duration, severity)
- Ophthalmological examination
- Sensory activity, grip strength, and motor activity assessments (when available)
- Terminal vaginal cytology

### Haematology
- Haematological tests with relevant baseline values

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Clinical biochemistry tests with relevant baseline values
- Circulating thyroid hormones: T4, T3, TSH (Required)
- Other hormone measures (Optional)

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Examination of all gross lesions.
- **Organ Weights:** Terminal organ weights and organ/body weight ratios.
- **Histopathology:** 
  - Full histopathology on control and high-dose groups; extended to other groups if changes observed.
  - Specific attention to testes (stage-specific changes, spermatid retention, etc.).
  - Evaluation of endocrine organs per OECD GD 106.
- **Sperm Measures:** Total cauda epididymal sperm number, motility, and morphology (Optional).
