## Purpose and Scope

The Hershberger Bioassay is a short-term in vivo screening test for androgen agonists, androgen antagonists, and 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors, based on changes in weight of five androgen-dependent accessory tissues in the castrate-peripubertal male rat. It is positioned at Level 3 of the OECD Conceptual Framework for Endocrine Disrupter Testing and Assessment, providing mechanistic data about (anti)androgenicity as a single endocrine mechanism.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily; more frequently when signs of toxicity are observed
- Observations include: mortality, morbidity, changes in behaviour, skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions, and autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, unusual respiratory pattern)
- All animals weighed daily to nearest 0.1 g, starting just prior to initiation of treatment
- Food consumption measured per cage (optional), expressed in grams per rat per day
- Moribund animals humanely euthanised; dead animals removed; both recorded with apparent reasons

### Haematology
- Not required in this guideline

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Not required as a standard panel
- Optional: Serum LH, FSH, and testosterone (T) — useful to detect steroid metabolism induction and hypothalamic-pituitary function
- Optional: Serum T4 and T3 — supplemental information on thyroid hormone homeostasis disruption

### Pathology
- All animals euthanised and exsanguinated approximately 24 hours after last dose; necropsy order randomized across groups
- Any pathological changes/visible lesions noted and reported
- Five androgen-dependent tissue weights are the primary required endpoints (VP, SVCG, LABC, COW, GP)
- All five tissues weighed fresh (unfixed), without blotting, to nearest 0.1 mg
- Optional organ weights: liver (to nearest 0.1 g), paired kidneys and paired adrenals (to nearest 0.1 mg)
- Preputial separation status recorded; if absent in any group, incidence compared to control via Fisher Exact test
