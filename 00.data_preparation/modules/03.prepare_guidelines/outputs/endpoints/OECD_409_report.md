## Purpose and Scope
This guideline describes a 90-day repeated dose oral toxicity study in non-rodent species (primarily dogs) to determine sub-chronic toxicity including major toxic effects, target organs, and the possibility of accumulation. It provides an estimate of a no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) for use in selecting dose levels for chronic studies and establishing safety criteria for human exposure. The guideline should only be used where effects in other studies require clarification in a non-rodent species, where toxicokinetic data indicate a specific non-rodent species is most relevant, or where other specific reasons justify non-rodent use.

## Key Observations
### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily, preferably at the same time each day (par. 20)
- Twice daily inspections for signs of morbidity and mortality (par. 20)
- Detailed weekly observations outside the home cage in a standard arena, covering: changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions and autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, respiratory pattern) (par. 21)
- Gait, posture, response to handling, clonic or tonic movements, stereotypes, and bizarre behaviour recorded (par. 21)
- Ophthalmological examination (ophthalmoscope) prior to dosing and at study termination; minimum in high dose and control groups; all animals if treatment-related ocular changes detected (par. 22)
- Body weight recorded at least once weekly (par. 23)
- Food consumption measured at least weekly; water consumption recorded weekly if substance administered in drinking water (par. 23)

### Haematology
- Haematocrit, haemoglobin concentration, erythrocyte count, total and differential leukocyte count, platelet count (par. 25)
- Measure of clotting potential: clotting time, prothrombin time, or thromboplastin time (par. 25)
- Evaluated at start of study, then monthly or midway through, and at end of study (par. 25)

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Electrolytes: calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium (par. 26; suggested)
- Carbohydrate metabolism: fasting glucose (par. 26; suggested)
- Liver function: alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, ornithine decarboxylase, gamma glutamyl transpeptidase, total bilirubin (par. 26; suggested)
- Kidney function: urea nitrogen, blood creatinine (par. 26; suggested)
- Other: albumin, total serum protein (par. 26; suggested)
- Additional as needed: lipids, hormones, acid/base balance, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase inhibition (par. 28; may be necessary)
- Urinalysis (timed urine): appearance, volume, osmolality or specific gravity, pH, protein, glucose, blood/blood cells (par. 27)
- Sampling at start, midway, and end of study; animals fasted prior to blood collection (par. 26-27)

### Pathology
- Full gross necropsy for all animals: external surface, all orifices, cranial, thoracic and abdominal cavities (par. 30; "shall")
- Wet organ weights: liver with gall bladder, kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, ovaries, uterus, thyroid (with parathyroids), thymus, spleen, brain and heart (par. 30)
- Tissues preserved for histopathology: all gross lesions, brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla/pons), spinal cord (cervical, mid-thoracic, lumbar), pituitary, eyes, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, oesophagus, salivary glands, stomach, small and large intestines (including Peyer's patches), liver, gall bladder, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, trachea and lungs, aorta, gonads, uterus, accessory sex organs, female mammary gland, prostate, urinary bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve (sciatic or tibial), bone marrow, skin (par. 31)
- Full histopathology on control and high dose groups; extended to all groups if treatment-related changes found in high dose group (par. 32)
- All gross lesions examined histopathologically (par. 33)
