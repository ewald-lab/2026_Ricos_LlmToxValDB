## Purpose and Scope

This guideline is designed to provide general information concerning the effects of a test substance on the integrity and performance of the male and female reproductive systems, including gonadal function, oestrus cycle, mating behaviour, conception, gestation, parturition, lactation, and weaning, as well as the growth and development of the offspring (F1 and F2 generations). It also provides preliminary data on prenatal and postnatal developmental toxicity.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Daily general clinical observations (morbidity, mortality, signs of toxicity).
- Detailed weekly examination of each animal.
- Behavioral changes and signs of difficult/prolonged parturition.
- Physical development of offspring (ear/eye opening, tooth eruption, hair growth).
- Functional investigations (motor activity, sensory function, reflex ontogeny) if not in separate studies.

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** All parental animals (P and F1), pups with abnormalities, and one randomly selected pup/sex/litter from F1/F2. Special attention to reproductive organs. Uteri examined for implantation sites.
- **Organ Weights:** 
  - Parental: Uterus, ovaries, testes, epididymides, prostate, seminal vesicles, brain, liver, kidneys, spleen, pituitary, thyroid, adrenals, target organs.
  - Offspring (selected): Brain, spleen, thymus.
- **Histopathology:**
  - Parental: Vagina, uterus/cervix, ovaries; testis, epididymis, seminal vesicles, prostate, coagulating gland; target organs. Full histopathology for high-dose and control groups.
  - Offspring: Grossly abnormal tissues and target organs from selected pups.
