## Purpose and Scope

OECD 416 is designed to provide information on the effects of a test substance on the integrity and performance of the male and female reproductive systems across two generations, covering gonadal function, oestrus cycle, mating behaviour, conception, gestation, parturition, lactation, and weaning. The study evaluates growth and development of both F1 and F2 offspring, and may provide preliminary data on prenatal and postnatal developmental toxicity and neonatal morbidity and mortality. The test substance is administered to parental (P) animals during growth, mating, gestation, and lactation, then continued to F1 offspring through adulthood, mating, and production of the F2 generation until F2 weaning.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observation each day; timing in gavage studies should account for anticipated peak period of effects after dosing
- Behavioural changes, signs of difficult or prolonged parturition, and all signs of toxicity recorded
- Additional detailed examination of each animal at least weekly (conveniently combined with weighing)
- Twice daily observations for morbidity and mortality (once daily on weekends when appropriate)
- Body weight: P and F1 parental animals shall be weighed on the first day of dosing and at least weekly thereafter; parental females weighed additionally at minimum on gestation days 0, 7, 14, and 20 or 21, during lactation on litter weighing days, and on day of sacrifice
- Food consumption shall be measured weekly at minimum during premating and gestation periods; water consumption measured weekly at minimum when test substance is administered in water
- Oestrous cycle length and normality evaluated by vaginal smears in P and F1 females prior to mating, and optionally during mating, until evidence of mating is found
- Sperm parameters assessed at termination for all P and F1 males: testis and epididymis weight recorded; enumeration of homogenisation-resistant testicular spermatids and cauda epididymal sperm reserves (subset of >=10 males/group); motility (% progressively motile); morphology (>=200 sperm/sample, classified as normal or abnormal)
- Offspring: each litter examined as soon as possible after delivery (lactation day 0) -- number and sex of pups, stillbirths, live births, presence of gross anomalies; live pups counted and weighed individually at birth (day 0 or 1) and on days 4, 7, 14, and 21 of lactation
- Physical developmental landmarks recorded (ear/eye opening, tooth eruption, hair growth); age of vaginal opening and preputial separation determined for F1 weanlings selected for mating
- Anogenital distance (AGD) measured at postnatal day 0 in F2 pups if triggered by alterations in F1 sex ratio or timing of sexual maturation
- Functional investigations (motor activity, sensory function, reflex ontogeny) of F1 offspring recommended if not performed in separate studies

### Pathology
- Full macroscopic examination at termination or death: all parental animals (P and F1), all pups with external abnormalities/clinical signs, and at least one randomly selected pup/sex/litter from both F1 and F2 generations
- Uteri of all primiparous females examined for presence and number of implantation sites
- **Organ weights (P and F1 parental animals):** uterus, ovaries, testes, epididymides (total and cauda, weighed individually), prostate, seminal vesicles with coagulating glands and fluids (as one unit), brain, liver, kidneys, spleen, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal glands, known target organs
- **Organ weights (F1/F2 pups selected for necropsy):** brain, spleen, thymus
- **Histopathology (parental P and F1):** vagina, uterus with cervix, ovaries (appropriate fixative); one testis (Bouin's or comparable fixative), one epididymis, seminal vesicles, prostate, coagulating gland; previously identified target organs; full histopathology of high dose and control groups; examination of P animal ovaries optional
- Detailed testicular histopathology: Bouin's fixative, paraffin embedding, transverse sections (4-5 um); evaluation of retained spermatids, missing germ cell layers, multinucleated giant cells, spermatogenic cell sloughing
- Epididymis histopathology: longitudinal section covering caput, corpus, and cauda; evaluated for leukocyte infiltration, cell type changes, aberrant cell types, sperm phagocytosis
- Quantitative evaluation of primordial follicles conducted for F1 females; comparison of treated and control ovaries
- Weanlings: grossly abnormal tissue and target organs from pups with abnormalities or clinical signs, and at least one randomly selected pup/sex/litter from F1 and F2 not selected for mating, fixed for histopathology with emphasis on reproductive organs
