## Purpose and Scope

This revised Test Guideline 412 is designed to fully characterize test chemical toxicity by the inhalation route for a subacute duration (28 days) and to provide robust data for quantitative inhalation risk assessments. The primary impetus for this revision was to accommodate the testing of nanomaterials and reflect the evolving state-of-the-science for testing inhaled gases, vapours, and aerosols. It specifically addresses study design dependencies on the physical form of the test chemical and lung retention characteristics.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   General clinical signs (morbidity, mortality, distress) observed at least once daily.
*   Body weight measurements recorded weekly.
*   Food consumption (if applicable for whole-body exposure).
*   Water consumption (if applicable).
*   Ophthalmological examination (optional/recommended if systemic effects are suspected).
*   Pulmonary function tests (optional, typically in range-finding or specific studies).

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Examination of all major organs and tissues.
*   **Organ Weights:** Absolute and relative weights of selected organs (e.g., lungs, liver, kidneys, adrenals, thyroid, thymus, spleen, brain, heart, pituitary, prostate, seminal vesicles, ovaries, uterus, testes, epididymides).
*   **Histopathology:**
    *   Mandatory: Left lung (for histopathology).
    *   Mandatory: Lungs-associated lymph nodes (LALN) if lung burden is measured.
    *   Recommended/Optional: Other target organs based on clinical signs or toxicokinetic data.
*   **Bronchoalveolar Lavage (BALF):** Required for all test chemicals. Analysis includes cell counts, differential, protein, enzymes (LDH, ALP), cytokines, etc.
*   **Lung Burden:** Required if the test chemical is likely to be retained in the lung (poorly soluble particles/nanomaterials).

### Footnotes

*   **Clinical Observations:** Moribund animals should be humanely sacrificed; criteria defined in GD 19.
*   **BALF Analysis:** Required at exposure termination (PEO-1) and additional PEOs if planned. In Option B, recovery group males are used for lung burden, so BALF is performed only on females in that group.
*   **Lung Burden:** Measurements are performed within 24h after exposure termination (PEO-1) and at additional PEOs (PEO-2, PEO-3) if clearance kinetics need evaluation. Preferably performed in males due to higher minute volume.
*   **Study Design:** Two options exist: Option A (general) and Option B (for chemicals likely to be retained in the lung). Option B requires satellite groups for lung burden measurements.
