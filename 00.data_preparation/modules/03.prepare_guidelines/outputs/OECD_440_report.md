## Purpose and Scope

The OECD Test Guideline 440 describes a short-term in vivo screening assay to identify substances with potential oestrogenic activity. It serves as an in vivo assay providing data about a single endocrine mechanism (oestrogenicity) within the OECD Conceptual Framework for Testing and Assessment of Endocrine Disrupting Chemicals. The bioassay evaluates the ability of a chemical to elicit biological activities consistent with agonists or antagonists of natural oestrogens, primarily by measuring the increase in uterine weight in immature or ovariectomized female rodents.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   General clinical observations made at least once daily (more frequently if signs of toxicity observed).
*   Observation for mortality, morbidity, and general clinical signs including changes in behaviour, skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions, and autonomic activity (e.g., lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, respiratory pattern).

### Pathology
*   **Necropsy:** Performed approximately 24 hours after the last dose.
*   **Uterus Weight Measurement:** Wet uterine weight and blotted uterine weight measured to the nearest 0.1 mg.
*   **Histopathology (Optional):** Uterus may be fixed in 10% neutral buffered formalin and examined histopathologically after H&E staining. Vagina may also be investigated. Morphometric measurement of endometrial epithelium may be done.
*   **Ovarian Stubs:** In ovariectomized animals, ovarian stubs examined for evidence of remaining ovarian tissue.
*   **Vaginal Status:** In immature animals, vagina examined for opening status before dissection.
