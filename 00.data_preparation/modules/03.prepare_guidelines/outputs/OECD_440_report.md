## Purpose and Scope

This guideline describes a short-term screening test to identify substances with potential oestrogenic activity (agonists or antagonists) in rodents. It is based on the increase in uterine weight or uterotrophic response following administration of the test substance. The assay serves as an in vivo screening tool within the OECD Conceptual Framework for Endocrine Disrupting Chemicals (Level 3), providing data on a single endocrine mechanism (oestrogenicity). Two methods are described: the immature non-ovariectomised method and the ovariectomised adult female method.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   Daily observation for clinical signs of toxicity or distress.
*   Monitoring of body weight (daily for immature; at start and necropsy for ovx-adult).
*   Food consumption monitoring (especially important due to phytooestrogen content in diet).

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Examination of ovarian stubs (for ovx-adult) to ensure no residual ovarian tissue.
*   **Organ Weights:** Absolute and relative uterine weights (wet weight) are the primary endpoint.
*   **Histopathology:** Optional examination of the vagina (keratinization/cornification) to analyze the origin of the response.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Mortality and morbidity monitoring (at least once daily).
- General clinical signs: behaviour, skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes.
- Secretions and excretions.
- Autonomic activity: lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, unusual respiratory patterns.

### Body Weight and Food Consumption
- **Daily Body Weight:** Required for all animals (nearest 0.1 g).
- **Food Consumption:** Optional measurement per cage (grams/rat/day).

### Pathology and Necropsy
- **Dissection:** Standardized removal of uterus, ovaries (if present), bladder, and ureters.
- **Vaginal Exam:** Status of vaginal opening checked in immature animals.
- **Ovarian Stubs:** Examined in ovariectomised animals to ensure complete removal.
- **Histopathology (Optional):** Uterus and vagina may be fixed in 10% NBF for H&E staining; morphometry of endometrial epithelium allowed.
