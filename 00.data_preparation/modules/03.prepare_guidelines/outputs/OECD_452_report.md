## Purpose and Scope

This guideline provides information on the possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated exposure over a considerable part of the lifespan of the species used. It aims to identify chronic toxicity, target organs, dose-response relationships, and no-observed-adverse effect levels (NOAEL). The study is designed primarily for rodents but principles apply to non-rodents with appropriate modifications.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Morbidity/mortality checks at least twice daily.
- General clinical observations at least once daily.
- Detailed clinical observations (skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, autonomic activity, gait, posture, behavior) prior to first exposure, end of week 1, and monthly thereafter.
- Ophthalmological examination prior to first administration and preferably at termination (at least high dose and control groups).
- Neurotoxicity assessments (sensory reactivity) if indicated by previous studies.

### Haematology
- Conducted at interim kills (if applicable) and at termination.
- Parameters include standard haematological determinations (specific list not detailed in this excerpt but implied as "required" for thorough evaluation).

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Conducted at interim kills (if applicable) and at termination.
- Includes measurements relevant to organ function (liver, kidney, etc.).

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** All animals dying during the test and surviving animals at conclusion.
- **Organ Weights:** Absolute and relative weights of selected organs (specific list not fully enumerated in this excerpt but standard practice applies).
- **Histopathology:** Examination of tissues from control and high-dose groups, plus any tissues showing gross lesions.
- **Interim Kills:** May undergo same pathological investigations as main study animals.
