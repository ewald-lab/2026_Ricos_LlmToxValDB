## Purpose and Scope

This guideline investigates effects on a broad variety of potential toxicity targets following repeated oral exposure over 28 days. It provides information on health hazards likely to arise from such exposure, including effects on the nervous, immune, and endocrine systems. It is intended for hazard identification and risk assessment, serving as an alternative to a 90-day study when production volumes are low or as a preliminary to longer-term studies.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   General clinical observations at least once daily (preferably same time).
*   Morbidity and mortality observed at least twice daily.
*   Detailed clinical observations once before first exposure and at least once weekly thereafter (outside home cage, standard arena).
*   Signs include changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions/excretions, autonomic activity, gait, posture, response to handling, clonic/tonic movements, stereotypies, and bizarre behavior.
*   Sensory reactivity to stimuli (auditory, visual, proprioceptive), grip strength, and motor activity assessment in the fourth exposure week (optional if preliminary to 90-day study).

### Haematology
*   Haematocrit
*   Haemoglobin concentrations
*   Erythrocyte count
*   Reticulocytes
*   Total and differential leucocyte count
*   Platelet count
*   Measure of blood clotting time/potential
*   Methaemoglobin concentration and Heinz bodies (if oxidising properties suspected)

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Sodium
*   Potassium
*   Glucose
*   Total cholesterol
*   Urea
*   Creatinine
*   Total protein
*   Albumin
*   Enzymes indicative of hepatocellular effects (e.g., alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase, γ-glutamyl trans-peptidase, glutamate dehydrogenase)
*   Bile acids
*   Additional enzymes and bilirubin (if useful)
*   Urinalysis (optional): appearance, volume, osmolality/specific gravity, pH, protein, glucose, blood/blood cells
*   Calcium, phosphate, triglycerides, cholinesterase (if relevant to chemical class)

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities and contents.
*   **Organ Weights (Required):** Liver, kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, prostate + seminal vesicles with coagulating glands, thymus, spleen, brain, heart.
*   **Organ Weights (Optional):** Paired ovaries, uterus (including cervix), thyroid (after fixation).
*   **Histopathology:** Full histopathology on preserved organs/tissues of all control and high-dose animals; extended to other dose groups if treatment-related changes observed in high dose. Includes all gross lesions.
*   **Tissues Preserved:** Brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, pons), spinal cord, eye, stomach, small/large intestines (incl. Peyer's patches), liver, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, thymus, thyroid, trachea, lungs, gonads (testis/ovaries), accessory sex organs (uterus/cervix, epididymides, prostate/seminal vesicles), vagina, urinary bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve, skeletal muscle, bone (with marrow).
