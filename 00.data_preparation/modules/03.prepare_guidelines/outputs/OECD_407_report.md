## Purpose and Scope

This guideline investigates effects on a very broad variety of potential targets of toxicity, including the nervous, immune, and endocrine systems, following repeated oral exposure over a relatively limited period (28 days). It provides information on possible health hazards, identifies chemicals with neurotoxic potential or those interfering with thyroid physiology, and may provide data on reproductive organs and immunological effects. The results are intended for hazard identification, risk assessment, and selection of concentrations for longer-term studies.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily (morbidity/mortality twice daily).
- Detailed clinical observations outside the home cage at least once weekly (skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, autonomic activity, gait, posture, stereotypies).
- Functional observations in the fourth week: sensory reactivity, grip strength, and motor activity assessment.
- Oestrus cycle determination via vaginal smears (optional).

### Haematology
- Haematocrit
- Haemoglobin concentrations
- Erythrocyte count
- Reticulocytes
- Total and differential leucocyte count
- Platelet count
- Blood clotting time/potential
- Methaemoglobin concentration and Heinz bodies (if oxidising properties suspected)

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Sodium, Potassium, Glucose
- Total cholesterol, Urea, Creatinine
- Total protein and Albumin
- Hepatocellular enzymes: Alanine aminotransferase (ALT), Aspartate aminotransferase (AST), Alkaline phosphatase (ALP), γ-glutamyl trans-peptidase (GGT), Glutamate dehydrogenase
- Bile acids
- Additional enzymes, Bilirubin, Calcium, Phosphate, Triglycerides, Specific hormones, Cholinesterase (case-by-case basis)
- Thyroid hormones (T3, T4) and TSH (optional, if indicated)

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, all orifices, and cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities.
- **Organ Weights (Required):** Liver, kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, prostate + seminal vesicles with coagulating glands, thymus, spleen, brain, heart.
- **Organ Weights (Optional):** Paired ovaries, uterus (including cervix), thyroid (after fixation).
- **Histopathology:** Full histopathology on control and high dose groups for preserved tissues (brain, spinal cord, eye, stomach, intestines, liver, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, thymus, thyroid, trachea, lungs, gonads, accessory sex organs, vagina, urinary bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve, skeletal muscle, bone, bone marrow).
- **Endocrine Sensitive Tissues:** Gonads, accessory sex organs, pituitary, male mammary gland, thyroid, adrenal gland.

### Footnotes

- *Note on Prostate:* Care must be exercised when trimming the prostate complex to avoid puncture of the fluid filled seminal vesicles. Alternatively, seminal vesicles and prostate may be trimmed and weighed after fixation.
- *Note on Thyroid:* Thyroid weight could be determined after fixation. Trimming should also be done very carefully and only after fixation to avoid tissue damage.
- *Note on Testes:* Recommended that testes be fixed by immersion in Bouin’s or modified Davidson’s fixative. The tunica albuginea must be gently and shallowly punctured at the both poles of the organ with a needle to permit rapid penetration of the fixative.
- *Note on Hormones:* Time of sacrifice, method of sacrifice, and test kits may influence variability. Control coefficients of variation should be below 25 for T3/T4 and below 35 for TSH.
- *Note on Functional Observations:* May be omitted when the study is conducted as a preliminary study to a subsequent subchronic (90-day) study.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Signs of toxicity (including onset, duration, and severity)
- Sensory activity
- Grip strength
- Motor activity assessments
- Body weight changes
- Food consumption
- Water consumption (if applicable)

### Haematology
- Haematological tests with relevant baseline values

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Clinical biochemistry tests with relevant baseline values

### Pathology
- **Gross Lesions**: All gross lesions must be examined.
- **Histopathology**: Full histopathology required for control and high-dose groups. Extended to other groups if treatment-related changes are found in the high dose.
- **Satellite Groups**: Histopathology required on tissues/organs showing effects.
- **Sample Preservation**: Optional cryopreservation of excess plasma/tissues for omics analysis.
