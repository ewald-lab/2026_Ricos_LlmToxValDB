## Purpose and Scope

This guideline provides information on the possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated exposure over a period of rapid growth and into young adulthood. It identifies major toxic effects, indicates target organs, assesses the possibility of accumulation, and estimates a no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) for selecting doses in chronic studies and establishing human safety criteria. It is intended for use when clarification in a second non-rodent species is needed or when toxicokinetic data justifies a specific non-rodent choice.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily.
- Detailed clinical observations at least once prior to first exposure and weekly thereafter (outside home cage).
- Signs recorded: skin/fur changes, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions/excretions, autonomic activity (lacrimation, pilo-erection), gait/posture, clonic/tonic movements, stereotypes, bizarre behavior.
- Ophthalmological examination prior to dosing and at termination (all animals preferred; minimum high dose and control).

### Haematology
- Haematocrit
- Haemoglobin concentration
- Erythrocyte count
- Total and differential leukocyte count
- Platelet count
- Clotting potential (clotting time, prothrombin time, or thromboplastin time)
- *Timing:* Start, monthly/midway, and end of study.

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Electrolytes: Calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium
- Carbohydrate metabolism: Fasting glucose
- Liver function: Alanine aminotransferase (ALT), Aspartate aminotransferase (AST), Gamma glutamyl transpeptidase (GGT), Total bilirubin, Total serum protein, Albumin
- Kidney function: Urea nitrogen, Blood creatinine
- Other: Ornithine decarboxylase
- *Timing:* Start, monthly/midway, and end of study.

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities.
- **Organ Weights:** Liver (with gall bladder), kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, ovaries, uterus, thyroid (with parathyroids), thymus, spleen, brain, heart.
- **Histopathology:** Full histopathology on preserved organs/tissues for control and high-dose groups (extended to other groups if treatment-related changes observed).
- **Preserved Tissues:** Brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla/pons), spinal cord, pituitary, eyes, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, oesophagus, salivary glands, stomach, intestines (including Peyer's patches), liver, gall bladder, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, trachea, lungs, aorta, gonads, uterus, accessory sex organs, mammary gland, prostate, urinary bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve, bone marrow, skin.
