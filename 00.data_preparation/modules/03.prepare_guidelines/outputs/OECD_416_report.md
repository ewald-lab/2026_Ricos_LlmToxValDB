## Purpose and Scope

This guideline is designed to provide general information concerning the effects of a test substance on the integrity and performance of the male and female reproductive systems, including gonadal function, oestrus cycle, mating behaviour, conception, gestation, parturition, lactation, and weaning. It also assesses the growth and development of the offspring (F1 and F2 generations) and may provide preliminary data on prenatal and postnatal developmental toxicity.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observation daily (including signs of toxicity, difficult/prolonged parturition).
- Detailed examination at least weekly.
- Morbidity and mortality checks twice daily (or once daily on weekends).
- Body weight measurements: Parental animals (weekly, plus specific days during gestation/lactation); Offspring (at birth, days 4, 7, 14, 21).
- Food consumption measured weekly (pre-mating and gestation).
- Water consumption measured weekly if administered via water.
- Oestrous cycle evaluation (vaginal smears) in P and F1 females prior to mating.
- Physical development of offspring (ear/eye opening, tooth eruption, hair growth).
- Functional investigations (motor activity, sensory function, reflex ontogeny) recommended if not in separate studies.
- Age at vaginal opening and preputial separation determination.
- Anogenital distance measurement (if triggered by alterations in sex ratio or maturation timing).

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** All parental animals (P and F1), pups with abnormalities, and one randomly selected pup/sex/litter from F1 and F2. Special attention to reproductive organs. Examination of uteri for implantation sites in primiparous females.
- **Organ Weights:**
  - Parental (P & F1): Uterus, ovaries, testes, epididymides (total and cauda), prostate, seminal vesicles with coagulating glands, brain, liver, kidneys, spleen, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal glands, and target organs.
  - Offspring (F1/F2 selected): Brain, spleen, thymus.
- **Histopathology:**
  - Parental (P & F1): Vagina, uterus with cervix, ovaries (optional for P), one testis, one epididymis, seminal vesicles, prostate, coagulating gland, and target organs. Full histopathology required for high dose and control groups.
  - Offspring: Grossly abnormal tissue and target organs from selected pups.
