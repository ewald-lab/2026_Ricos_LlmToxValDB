Your task is to evaluate whether a candidate ontology term represents a phenotype that could plausibly be detected, recorded, scored, measured, or inferred from a toxicology guideline endpoint.

CRITICAL RULES FOR EVALUATION:

1. ACCEPT SPECIFIC OUTCOMES: If the candidate is a specific outcome (e.g., "increased liver weight", "decreased liver weight", "abnormal liver morphology") and the endpoint is the general measurement (e.g., "liver weight"), you MUST ACCEPT the candidate.
