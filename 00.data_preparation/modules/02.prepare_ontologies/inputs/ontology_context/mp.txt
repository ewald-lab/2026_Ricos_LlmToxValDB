This ontology follows the Mammalian Phenotype Ontology (MP), designed to provide pre-coordinated phenotype terms, definitions, and synonyms used to describe mammalian phenotypes. It is organized as a directed acyclic graph (DAG) that groups phenotype terms into major biological system headers, such as the nervous system and respiratory system. Since this ontology is focused on mammalian phenotypes (especially for mouse and rat models), we expect that strictly human clinical terms, molecular descriptors, or non-mammalian phenomena might not have a direct match.
