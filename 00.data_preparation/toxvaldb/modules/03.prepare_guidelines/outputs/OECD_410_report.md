## Purpose and Scope

This guideline provides a method for determining subchronic dermal toxicity after initial acute testing information is obtained. It assesses possible health hazards arising from repeated dermal exposures over a limited period (21 or 28 days). The study helps identify target organs, determine no-effect levels, and select dose levels for longer-term studies.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   Careful clinical examination at least once each day.
*   Daily observations for signs of toxicity including changes in skin and fur, eyes, mucous membranes, respiratory, circulatory, autonomic, central nervous system, somatomotor activity, and behaviour patterns.
*   Monitoring for mortality, morbidity, and weight loss.

### Haematology
*   Haematocrit
*   Haemoglobin concentration
*   Erythrocyte count
*   Total leucocyte count
*   Differential leucocyte count
*   Measure of clotting potential (clotting time, prothrombin time, thromboplastin time, or platelet count)

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Liver function parameters: Serum glutamic-pyruvic transaminase (ALT), serum glutamic-oxaloacetic transaminase (AST), ornithine decarboxylase, gamma glutamyl transpeptidase.
*   Kidney function parameters: Urea nitrogen, blood creatinine.
*   General parameters: Calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium, fasting glucose, albumen, total bilirubin, total serum protein.
*   Additional optional tests: Lipids, hormones, acid/base balance, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase activity.

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Full examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities. Wet weights of liver, kidneys, adrenals, and testes. Preservation of normal/treated skin, liver, kidney, and target organs.
*   **Histopathology:** Examination of preserved organs/tissues from high-dose and control groups (extended to other groups/satellite groups if necessary). Emphasis on organs showing effects or gross lesions.
