## Purpose and Scope

The 90-day study provides information on possible health hazards likely to arise from repeated exposure over a period of rapid growth and into young adulthood. It identifies major toxic effects, target organs, potential accumulation, and estimates the no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL), which informs chronic study design and human safety criteria. This guideline is primarily used where clarification in a second non-rodent species is needed or when toxicokinetics justify a specific non-rodent choice.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- General clinical observations at least once daily (preferably same time).
- Inspection for morbidity and mortality at least twice daily.
- Detailed clinical observations at least once prior to first exposure and once weekly thereafter.
- Signs include changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions/excretions, autonomic activity (lacrimation, pilo-erection, pupil size, respiratory pattern), gait, posture, response to handling, clonic/tonic movements, stereotypes, or bizarre behavior.

### Haematology
- Haematocrit
- Haemoglobin concentration
- Erythrocyte count
- Total leukocyte count
- Differential leukocyte count
- Platelet count
- Clotting potential measures (clotting time, prothrombin time, or thromboplastin time)

### Clinical Biochemistry
- Electrolytes: Calcium, phosphorus, chloride, sodium, potassium
- Carbohydrate metabolism: Fasting glucose
- Liver function: Alanine aminotransferase (ALT), Aspartate aminotransferase (AST), Ornithine decarboxylase, Gamma glutamyl transpeptidase (GGT)
- Kidney function: Urea nitrogen, Blood creatinine
- Other: Total bilirubin, Total serum protein, Albumin
- Additional considerations: Lipids, hormones, acid/base balance, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase inhibition (as necessary)

### Pathology
- **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities.
- **Organ Weights:** Wet weight of liver (with gall bladder), kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, ovaries, uterus, thyroid (with parathyroids), thymus, spleen, brain, heart.
- **Histopathology:** Preservation of specified tissues including gross lesions, brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla/pons), spinal cord, pituitary, eyes, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, oesophagus, salivary glands, stomach, small/large intestines (including Peyer's patches), liver, gall bladder, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, trachea, lungs, aorta, gonads, uterus, accessory sex organs, female mammary gland, prostate, urinary bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve, bone marrow, and skin. Full histopathology on all preserved organs/tissues in control and high-dose groups; extended to other groups if treatment-related changes observed.
