## Purpose and Scope

This guideline is designed for the testing of a broad range of chemicals, including pesticides and industrial chemicals, to provide information on possible health hazards arising from repeated exposure over a considerable part of the lifespan. It aims to identify chronic toxicity, target organs, dose-response relationships, and the no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) or point of departure for Benchmark Dose (BMD) estimation. The study can also predict chronic toxicity effects at human exposure levels and test hypotheses regarding mode of action.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   Morbidity and mortality checks at the beginning and end of each day.
*   General clinical observations at least once daily.
*   Detailed clinical observations prior to first exposure, after one week, and monthly thereafter.
*   Signs include changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions/excretions, autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, respiratory pattern), gait, posture, response to handling, clonic/tonic movements, stereotypies, and bizarre behavior.
*   Ophthalmological examination prior to administration and at termination (preferably all animals, at least high dose and control). Frequency increased if structural analysis suggests ocular toxicity.
*   Optional neurotoxicity assessments (sensory reactivity, grip strength, motor activity) if indicated by previous studies.
*   Optional immunotoxicity investigations if indicated by previous studies.

### Haematology
*   Performed on at least 10 male and 10 female animals per group at 3, 6, and 12 months, and at termination (if >12 months).
*   Parameters: Total leukocyte count, differential leukocyte count, erythrocyte count, platelet count, haemoglobin concentration, haematocrit (packed cell volume), mean corpuscular volume (MCV), mean corpuscular haemoglobin (MCH), mean corpuscular haemoglobin concentration (MCHC), prothrombin time, activated partial thromboplastin time.
*   Additional parameters as appropriate: Heinz bodies, atypical erythrocyte morphology, methaemoglobin, reticulocyte counts, bone marrow cytology (if haematopoietic effect suspected).

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Performed on blood samples from at least 10 male and 10 female animals per group at same intervals as haematology.
*   Parameters: Glucose, urea (urea nitrogen), creatinine, total protein, albumin, calcium, sodium, potassium, total cholesterol.
*   Hepatocellular evaluation: At least two tests (alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, glutamate dehydrogenase, total bile acids).
*   Hepatobiliary evaluation: At least two tests (alkaline phosphatase, gamma glutamyl transferase, 5'-nucleotidase, total bilirubin, total bile acids).
*   Other parameters as appropriate: Fasting triglycerides, specific hormones, cholinesterase.
*   Urinalysis determinations on same intervals: Appearance, volume, osmolality/specific gravity, pH, total protein, glucose. Others: ketone, urobilinogen, bilirubin, occult blood.

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed gross necropsy on all animals (except interim/satellite groups where restricted measures apply). Examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities.
*   **Organ Weights:** Collected from all animals (excluding those excluded by necropsy restrictions). Mandatory weights: Adrenals, brain, epididymides, heart, kidneys, liver, ovaries, spleen, testes, thyroid (post-fixation), uterus. Optional in mice: Adrenal glands.
*   **Histopathology:** Minimum examinations include all tissues from high dose and control groups, all tissues from animals dying/killed during study, all tissues showing macroscopic abnormalities, and target tissues/tissues with treatment-related changes from all dose groups. Paired organs must both be examined.
*   **Tissues Preserved:** All gross lesions, heart, pancreas, stomach (forestomach/glandular), adrenal gland, ileum, parathyroid gland [teeth], aorta, jejunum, peripheral nerve, testis, brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla/pons), kidney, pituitary, thymus, caecum, lacrimal gland (exorbital), prostate, thyroid, cervix, liver, rectum [tongue], coagulating gland, lung, salivary gland, trachea, colon, lymph nodes (superficial/deep), seminal vesicle, urinary bladder, duodenum, mammary gland (obligatory females, if visible males), skeletal muscle, uterus (including cervix), epididymis [upper respiratory tract], skin [ureter], eye (including retina), oesophagus, spinal cord (cervical, mid-thoracic, lumbar) [urethra] [femur with joint] [olfactory bulb], spleen, vagina, gall bladder (non-rat), ovary [sternum], section of bone marrow/fresh aspirate, Harderian gland.
