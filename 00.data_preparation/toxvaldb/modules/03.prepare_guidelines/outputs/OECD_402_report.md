## Purpose and Scope

This guideline describes a stepwise procedure for the acute dermal toxicity testing of chemicals using the Fixed Dose Procedure (FDP). It is designed to estimate toxicity ranges and classify chemicals according to the Globally Harmonised System (GHS) while minimizing animal use. The test is intended for human health assessment, with specific considerations for waiving testing based on oral toxicity data or physicochemical properties.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   Observed immediately after dosing, during the first 30 minutes, periodically during the first 24 hours (with special attention to the first 2–6 hours), and daily thereafter for at least 14 days.
*   Includes changes in skin and fur, eyes and mucous membranes, respiratory, circulatory, autonomic and central nervous systems, somatomotor activity, and behaviour pattern.
*   Specific attention to tremors, convulsions, salivation, diarrhoea, lethargy, sleep, and coma.
*   Treatment site observed at 24, 48, and 72 hours after removal of test chemical using Draize criteria.

### Haematology
*   Not required as part of the standard protocol for this acute guideline.

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Not required as part of the standard protocol for this acute guideline.

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** All test animals (including those that die or are killed for humane reasons) must be subjected to gross necropsy. All gross pathological changes recorded.
*   **Histopathology:** Microscopic examination of organs showing evidence of gross pathology, or of the treatment site, in animals surviving 24 or more hours after initial dosing may be considered if it yields useful information.
