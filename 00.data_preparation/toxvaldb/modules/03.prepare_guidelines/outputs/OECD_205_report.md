## Purpose and Scope

This guideline describes a method for determining the median lethal concentration (LC50) of a chemical in the diet for avian species. It involves feeding birds a diet containing the test substance at various concentrations for five days, followed by a recovery period on basal diet. The test is used to assess acute dietary toxicity and establish mortality rates associated with specific dietary concentrations.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
- Signs of intoxication and other abnormal behaviour: recorded twice on day 1, then daily thereafter.
- Mortality: recorded twice on day 1, then daily thereafter.

### Haematology

### Clinical Biochemistry

### Pathology
- Individual weights of all birds that die during the test are recorded.
- Gross necropsy is implied by the recording of deaths and individual weights, but detailed histopathology or organ weight measurements are not required endpoints in this acute dietary test.
