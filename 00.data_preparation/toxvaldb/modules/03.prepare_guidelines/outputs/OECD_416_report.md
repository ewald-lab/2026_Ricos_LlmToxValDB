## Purpose and Scope

This guideline is designed to provide general information concerning the effects of a test substance on the integrity and performance of the male and female reproductive systems, including gonadal function, oestrus cycle, mating behaviour, conception, gestation, parturition, lactation, and weaning, as well as the growth and development of the offspring. It assesses both the P generation and the F1 generation's reproductive integrity and the growth/development of the F2 generation. It also provides preliminary data on prenatal and postnatal developmental toxicity and neonatal morbidity/mortality.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   Daily general clinical observation (timing considers peak effect period for gavage).
*   Record behavioural changes, signs of difficult/prolonged parturition, and all signs of toxicity.
*   Detailed examination conducted at least weekly (conveniently during weighing).
*   Twice daily (or once daily on weekends/appropriate days) observation for morbidity and mortality.

### Haematology
*   *Note: Specific haematological parameters are not explicitly mandated in the body text of TG 416 as they are in repeated-dose guidelines like TG 407/408. However, sperm parameters are detailed.*
*   Enumeration of homogenisation-resistant spermatids (testis).
*   Enumeration of cauda epididymides sperm reserves.
*   Sperm motility evaluation (percentage of progressively motile sperm).
*   Sperm morphology evaluation (classification as normal or abnormal, e.g., fusion, isolated heads, misshapen heads/tails).

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   *Note: Specific clinical biochemistry panels (e.g., ALT, AST, Creatinine) are not explicitly mandated in the body text of TG 416.*

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** All parental animals (P and F1), pups with external abnormalities/clinical signs, and at least one randomly selected pup/sex/litter from F1 and F2 generations examined macroscopically. Special attention to reproductive organs. Uteri of primiparous females examined for implantation sites.
*   **Organ Weights:**
    *   Parental (P and F1): Uterus, ovaries, testes, epididymides (total and cauda), prostate, seminal vesicles with coagulating glands (as one unit), brain, liver, kidneys, spleen, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal glands, and known target organs.
    *   Offspring (F1/F2 random pup): Brain, spleen, thymus.
*   **Histopathology:**
    *   **Parental:** Vagina, uterus with cervix, ovaries, one testis, one epididymis, seminal vesicles, prostate, coagulating gland, and previously identified target organs. Full histopathology required for high-dose and control groups. Examination of ovaries of P animals is optional. Detailed testicular histopathology (retained spermatids, germ cell layers, etc.) and epididymal evaluation (leukocyte infiltration, cell types) required. Quantitative evaluation of primordial follicles in F1 females recommended.
    *   **Weanlings:** Grossly abnormal tissue and target organs from pups with abnormalities/signs, plus one random pup/sex/litter from F1 and F2 generations. Full histopathological characterisation with emphasis on reproductive system.
