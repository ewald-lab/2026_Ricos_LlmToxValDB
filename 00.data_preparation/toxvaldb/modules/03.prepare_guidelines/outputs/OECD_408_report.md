## Purpose and Scope

This guideline provides a method for determining sub-chronic oral toxicity using repeated doses over at least 90 days to identify major toxic effects, target organs, and potential accumulation. It yields dose-related response data to estimate a No-Observed-Adverse-Effect Level (NOAEL) or point of departure for hazard assessment. The revised guideline places additional emphasis on endocrine-sensitive endpoints to improve the detection of potential endocrine activity, alongside neurological, immunological, and reproductive effects.

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   General clinical observations at least once daily (preferably same time).
*   Inspection for signs of morbidity and mortality at least twice daily.
*   Detailed clinical observations at least once prior to first exposure and once weekly thereafter (outside home cage).
*   Signs include changes in skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions/excretions, autonomic activity, gait, posture, response to handling, clonic/tonic movements, stereotypes, or bizarre behavior.
*   Ophthalmological examination prior to administration and at termination (all animals if changes detected; otherwise high dose and control groups).
*   Functional observations towards end of study (week 11+): sensory reactivity, grip strength, motor activity.

### Haematology
*   Haematocrit
*   Haemoglobin concentration
*   Erythrocyte count
*   Reticulocyte count
*   Total leukocyte count
*   Differential leukocyte count
*   Platelet count
*   Measure of blood clotting time/potential

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Sodium
*   Potassium
*   Glucose
*   Total cholesterol
*   High-density lipoproteins (HDL)
*   Low-density lipoproteins (LDL)
*   Urea / Blood urea nitrogen
*   Creatinine
*   Total protein
*   Albumin
*   Enzymes indicative of hepatocellular effects (e.g., ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, sorbitol dehydrogenase)
*   Additional enzymes/bile acids/bilirubin (if warranted)
*   Calcium, phosphorus, fasting triglycerides, specific hormones, methaemoglobin, cholinesterase (if suspected metabolic profile effects)
*   Serum total T4, T3, and TSH (Required)
*   Other hormones (Testosterone, oestradiol, FSH, LH) as case-by-case basis (Optional)

### Pathology
*   **Gross Necropsy:** Full detailed examination of external surface, orifices, cranial/thoracic/abdominal cavities.
*   **Organ Weights:** Liver, kidneys, adrenals, testes, epididymides, prostate + seminal vesicles with coagulating glands, uterus, ovaries, thymus, spleen, brain, heart, pituitary gland, thyroid gland.
*   **Histopathology:** Preservation and microscopic examination of specified tissues including gross lesions, brain, spinal cord, pituitary, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, oesophagus, salivary glands, stomach, intestines, liver, pancreas, kidneys, adrenals, spleen, heart, trachea, lungs, aorta, ovaries, uterus, cervix, vagina, testes, epididymides, prostate, seminal vesicles, coagulation glands, mammary glands, urinary bladder, gall bladder, lymph nodes, peripheral nerve, skeletal muscle, bone/marrow, skin, and eyes (if indicated).
*   **Reproductive Specifics:** Vaginal smears for oestrus cycle determination in females; testis/epididymis weights and histopathology in males; optional sperm morphology/motility/reserves.
