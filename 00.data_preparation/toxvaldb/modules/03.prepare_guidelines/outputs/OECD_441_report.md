## Purpose and Scope

The Hershberger Bioassay is a short-term in vivo screening assay used to detect chemicals with androgen agonist, androgen antagonist, or 5α-reductase inhibitor properties. It utilizes accessory tissues of the male reproductive tract in castrate-peripubertal male rats to evaluate biological activities consistent with these endocrine mechanisms. It is intended for use within the OECD Conceptual Framework for Testing and Assessment of Endocrine Disrupting Chemicals (Level 3).

## Key Observations

### Clinical Observations
*   General clinical observations at least once daily (more frequently if toxicity signs appear)
*   Monitoring for mortality, morbidity, and general clinical signs including changes in behavior, skin, fur, eyes, mucous membranes, secretions, excretions, and autonomic activity (lacrimation, piloerection, pupil size, respiratory pattern)

### Haematology
*   Not required by this guideline

### Clinical Biochemistry
*   Not required by this guideline

### Pathology
*   **Necropsy:** Full necropsy performed ~24 hours after last dose
*   **Target Tissue Weights (Required):** Fresh weights of five androgen-dependent tissues:
    *   Ventral prostate (VP)
    *   Seminal vesicles plus coagulating glands (SVCG)
    *   Levator ani plus bulbocavernosus muscle complex (LABC)
    *   Cowper’s glands (COW)
    *   Glans penis (GP)
*   **Optional Organ Weights:** Liver, paired kidneys, paired adrenals
*   **Histopathology:** Not explicitly required for routine screening, but pathological changes/visible lesions noted at necropsy
