This ontology follows the Adverse Outcome Pathway (AOP) Ontology, designed to capture the sequence of events from a molecular initiating event to an adverse in vivo outcome. It provides a structured vocabulary for describing toxicological processes and mechanisms that lead to adverse health effects in organisms or populations. More info at https://aopwiki.org/ Since this ontology is focused on adverse outcome pathways, we expect that some terms strictly related to specific structural phenotypes or isolated observations may not find a direct match.
