This ontology follows the TOXic Process Ontology (TXPO), designed to systematize a wide variety of terms involving toxicity courses and processes to elucidate the mechanism of toxicity for drug safety evaluations. It contains an is-a hierarchy organized into three layers: general terms, intermediate biomedical terms, and lower-layer toxicological terms. Notably, the first version of TXPO focuses heavily on liver toxicity. More info at https://toxpilot.nibiohn.go.jp Since this ontology focuses on toxicity courses and mechanisms, and specifically on liver toxicity, we expect that non-mechanistic descriptions or phenotypes unrelated to its core focus may not have a perfect match.
