This ontology follows the Mouse Pathology Ontology (MPATH), developed by the Pathbase consortium as a standardized vocabulary of experimental mouse pathology, organised under the root term "pathological entity" into an almost entirely is_a hierarchy of pathological processes and lesion types. It should be used to classify findings according to the histopathological lesion or pathological process observed, rather than the affected anatomical structure, the clinical symptom or the measurement reported. More info at https://obofoundry.org/ontology/mpath.html Since this ontology is focused on histopathology, we expect that clinical signs, organ weight or biochemical changes, and behavioural or mechanistic findings may not have a direct match.
