This ontology follows the Mondo Disease Ontology (MONDO), a global disease ontology that merges and harmonises terms from multiple disease resources (including OMIM, Orphanet, DOID and NCIt) into a single coherent hierarchy under the root term "disease". It should be used to classify findings according to the named disease or disorder they correspond to, rather than the individual sign, lesion or measurement observed. More info at https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/ Since this ontology is focused on defined disease entities, we expect that isolated toxicological observations, histopathological lesions and dose-response measurements that do not amount to a named disease may not have a direct match.
