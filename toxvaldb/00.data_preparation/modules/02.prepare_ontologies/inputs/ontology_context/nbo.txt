This ontology follows the Neuro Behavior Ontology (NBO), developed as a standardized vocabulary of behavioural processes and behavioural phenotypes, used here under the root term "behavioral phenotype", which groups abnormal behaviour into cognitive, emotional, kinesthetic, feeding, reproductive, rhythmic and social behaviour phenotypes, together with seizures and mood and psychotic disorders. It should be used to classify findings according to the abnormal behaviour or neurological sign observed, rather than the affected anatomical structure, the underlying lesion or the measurement reported. More info at https://obofoundry.org/ontology/nbo.html Since this ontology covers behaviour only and is small, we expect that most non-behavioural findings, such as histopathological lesions, organ weight or biochemical changes and systemic toxicity, may not have a direct match.
