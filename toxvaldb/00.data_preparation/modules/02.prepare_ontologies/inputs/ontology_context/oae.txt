This ontology follows the Ontology of Adverse Events (OAE), developed by the He group at the University of Michigan as a standardized vocabulary of adverse events that follow a medical intervention, organised under the root term "adverse event" into body systems such as cardiovascular, behavioral and neurological, immune system, digestive system and abnormal investigation result adverse events. It should be used to classify findings according to the adverse event they describe and the body system it affects, rather than the underlying disease mechanism, the anatomical structure alone or the dose at which it was observed. More info at https://obofoundry.org/ontology/oae.html Since this ontology is centred on clinical adverse events in humans, we expect that animal-specific histopathological lesions, organ weight changes and mechanistic or molecular findings may not have a direct match.
