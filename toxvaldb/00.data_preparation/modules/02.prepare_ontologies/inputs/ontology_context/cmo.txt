This ontology follows the Clinical Measurement Ontology (CMO), developed by the Rat Genome Database as a standardized vocabulary of the measurements and assays performed on an organism, organised under the root term "clinical measurement" into body systems such as blood, organ, renal/urinary and cardiovascular measurements. It should be used to classify findings according to the quantity that was measured, rather than the direction of the change, the resulting disease or the histopathological lesion observed. More info at https://obofoundry.org/ontology/cmo.html Since this ontology describes measurements rather than outcomes, we expect that named diseases, lesions and qualitative clinical signs may not have a direct match.
