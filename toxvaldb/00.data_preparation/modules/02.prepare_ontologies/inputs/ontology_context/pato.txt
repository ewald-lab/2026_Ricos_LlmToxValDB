This ontology follows the Phenotype And Trait Ontology (PATO), developed by the OBO Foundry as a standardized vocabulary of phenotypic qualities, organised under the root term "quality" into physical object qualities (morphology, size, mass, colour, functionality), process qualities and qualitative deviations from normal such as increased and decreased. It should be used to classify findings according to the kind of change observed, such as a change in size, shape, rate or amount, rather than the organ, the disease or the lesion in which it occurs. More info at https://obofoundry.org/ontology/pato.html Since this ontology describes qualities without the entity that bears them, we expect that named diseases, specific lesions and findings whose meaning depends on the affected organ may not have a direct match.
