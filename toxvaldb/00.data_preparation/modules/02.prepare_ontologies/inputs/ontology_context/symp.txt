This ontology follows the Symptom Ontology (SYMP), developed by the Disease Ontology group as a standardized vocabulary of the symptoms of human disease, organised under the root term "symptom". It should be used to classify findings according to the symptom or clinical sign observed, rather than the underlying disease, the affected anatomical structure or the measurement reported. More info at https://disease-ontology.org/ Since this ontology covers patient-level symptoms and is small and human-centred, we expect that histopathological lesions, dose-response measurements and animal-specific or mechanistic findings may not have a direct match.
