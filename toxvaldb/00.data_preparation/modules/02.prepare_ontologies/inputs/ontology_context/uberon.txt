This ontology follows Uberon, a cross-species anatomy ontology designed to represent anatomical structures, organs, and tissues in a species-agnostic way, integrating with other OBO ontologies. It should be used to classify phenotypes according to the anatomical entity or body site affected, rather than the nature of the finding itself. Since this ontology is focused purely on anatomical structures rather than phenotypic abnormalities or pathological processes, we expect that terms describing functional, behavioral, or mechanistic aspects of toxicity may not have a direct match.
