Expert Annotation: Toxicological Effects

Context of the ontology: This ontology follows the Histopathology Ontology (HPATH), designed to standardize the recording and exchange of pathology data. It provides a structured vocabulary for histopathological findings, lesions, and observations in tissues and organs, often used in preclinical and clinical toxicity studies. More info at https://etransafe.eu/histopathology-ontology/ Since this is an ontology focused on histhopatology, we expect that many terms will not have a match.
